One AI, two networks

A growing company in motion.

WormAI evaluates the map, builds point-to-point connections, and manages the vehicles that keep each route moving.

01 / AIR

Connect towns from above

Find suitable towns, place airports, and establish passenger services between them. Aircraft are selected to suit the route distance.

02 / RAIL

Move every kind of cargo

Build dedicated rail routes between industries, then renew trains and adapt train length as the business grows.

03 / MANAGE

Keep the fleet on track

Maintain and upgrade routes, replace old vehicles, and respond to the changing needs of a competitive game.

Ready to share the map?
